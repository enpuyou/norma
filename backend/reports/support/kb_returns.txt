## Cancellation Policy

### Subscription Cancellation
Can be done at any time through Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan.
- Cancellation takes effect at end of current billing period
- No prorated refund for monthly plans mid-period (exception: billing errors)
- Annual plans: cancellation within 14 days of renewal → full refund
- Data export: 30-day window post-cancellation to export data

### Hardware Returns (if applicable)
Return window: 30 days from delivery
Condition: must be in original packaging, no physical damage
RMA process: submit at returns.example.com, include order number
Refund: issued within 5 business days of return receipt inspection

## Enterprise Contract Cancellations
Governed by signed MSA/SOW terms. Cancellation requires written notice
per contractually specified notice period (typically 30-90 days).
Early termination fees may apply per contract terms.
Contact your Account Executive before initiating cancellation.

## Downgrades
Downgrade from higher tier is effective at next renewal date.
Features from higher tier remain accessible until renewal.
Some data limits become active immediately (e.g., storage quotas).

ESCALATION PATH
Customer requesting cancellation + high ARR (>$50K): flag to Customer Success
Dispute over cancellation refund: escalate to billing.escalations@example.com

PHARMA PATENT CLIFF ANALYSIS — 2026-2030
=========================================
Prepared: January 2026 | Classification: Public

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The pharmaceutical industry faces its largest patent cliff in history over
2026-2030, with an estimated $280B in branded drug revenue at risk from generic
and biosimilar competition. This analysis covers the major molecules at risk and
strategic responses by affected companies.

BIOSIMILAR MARKET
Biologics represent 40% of total global pharma revenue. Biosimilar penetration
remains lower than small molecule generics due to higher development costs and
physician/patient adoption friction. However, adalimumab biosimilars captured
35% of US market share within 18 months of launch, suggesting accelerating
uptake for future biosimilars.

STRATEGIC RESPONSES
- Merck: Pipeline transition to co-formulations; Keytruda + chemo
  combos may extend exclusivity
- AstraZeneca: Oncology pipeline deepening; Tagrisso successor compounds
- Pfizer: Oncology acquisition strategy post-Seagen integration

KEY RISK: Companies with >40% revenue concentration in patents expiring
2026-2028 face significant earnings pressure regardless of pipeline execution.

Sources: EvaluatePharma, FDA Purple Book, company filings

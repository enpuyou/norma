SEMICONDUCTOR SUPPLY CHAIN — Q4 2025 RESEARCH BRIEF
=====================================================
Prepared: January 2026 | Classification: Public

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Global semiconductor supply chain stabilized in Q4 2025 following 18 months of
constrained lead times. TSMC 3nm capacity utilization reached 94% driven by AI
accelerator demand. Samsung and Intel continued capacity investments.

KEY FINDINGS
1. Lead Times: Average lead times declined from 26 weeks (Q3 2025) to 18 weeks
   (Q4 2025) across logic and memory segments.

2. AI Accelerator Demand: H100/H200 GPU equivalents saw 340% YoY demand increase.
   NVIDIA, AMD, and custom silicon (Google TPU v5, Amazon Trainium 2) drove the
   majority of TSMC capacity allocation.

3. Geopolitical Risk: ASML EUV export restrictions continued to limit advanced
   logic capacity outside Taiwan and South Korea. This represents a structural
   supply concentration risk for 2026.

4. Inventory: Consumer electronics inventory digestion largely complete by Q4 2025.
   PC DRAM and NAND oversupply conditions eased; pricing recovered 15-22%.

RISK FACTORS
- Taiwan Strait geopolitical tension remains an extreme tail risk
- US CHIPS Act facility ramp-up slower than projected (2028 vs. 2027 timeline)
- Advanced packaging bottleneck (CoWoS) limits AI chip output despite fab capacity

OUTLOOK 2026
AI infrastructure buildout will sustain above-trend demand. Automotive and
industrial segments recovering from 2024 inventory correction. Overall sector
revenue growth estimate: 12-15% YoY.

Sources: SEMI Quarterly Report, IDC Supply Chain Tracker, company filings

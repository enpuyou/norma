## Common Billing Questions

### Q: How do I request a refund?
Refunds can be requested within 30 days of purchase. Submit a refund request
through the customer portal at help.example.com/refund. Processing time is
3-5 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

For subscription charges:
- Monthly subscriptions: prorated refund for unused days
- Annual subscriptions: refund eligible within 14 days of renewal
- One-time purchases: full refund within 30 days, partial after

### Q: Why was I charged twice?
Duplicate charges typically result from a payment retry after a temporary
network error. To verify: log in to your account → Billing → Transaction History.
If a duplicate charge is confirmed, file a billing dispute ticket and include
the transaction IDs. Refund issued within 2 business days.

### Q: How do I update my payment method?
Account Settings → Billing → Payment Methods → Add New Method.
Supports Visa, Mastercard, Amex, ACH bank transfer, and wire transfer (enterprise).

### Q: Invoice requests
PDF invoices auto-generate for all charges. Download from:
Account Settings → Billing → Invoices → Select date range.
For consolidated invoices across multiple accounts, contact billing@example.com.

### Q: Promotional code not applying
Ensure the code has not expired. Codes are case-sensitive. Codes for new
customers cannot be applied to existing accounts. One code per order.
